# Where you live

Source: Unit 2.11.

| Level    | English              | Deutsch                 |
| -------- | -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| informal | Where do you live?   | Wo wohnst du?           |
| neutral  | Where does she live? | Wo lebt sie?            |
| neutral  | I live in ...        | Ich wohne in...         |
| neutral  | She lives in ...     | Sie lebt in...          |
| neutral  | I was born in ...    | Ich bin in ... geboren. |

*wohnen* is to reside at an address. *leben* is to live in the broader sense. Both are regular.

*Ich bin ... geboren* uses *sein*, not *haben* — see `04-sein-to-be.md`.
