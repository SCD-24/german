# Articles and possessives

Sources: Unit 2.2, Unit 2.4.

## The definite article

| Gender    | Deutsch | English | Example    |
| --------- | ------- | ------- | ---------- |
| masculine | der     | the     | der Vater  |
| feminine  | die     | the     | die Mutter |
| neuter    | das     | the     | das Kind   |
| plural    | die     | the     | die Kinder |

Masculine and feminine differ, and plural reuses *die*. Learn every noun **with its article** — the
gender is not predictable from the meaning.

## mein / meine — my

| Used with         | Deutsch | Example                    |
| ----------------- | ------- | -------------------------- |
| masculine, neuter | mein    | mein Vater, mein Kind      |
| feminine, plural  | meine   | meine Mutter, meine Eltern |
