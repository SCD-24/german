# Who is that?

Source: Unit 2.3. This section repeats Unit 1.3 exactly.

| Level    | English       | Deutsch         |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| informal | Who are you?  | Wer bist du?    |
| formal   | Who are you?  | Wer sind Sie?   |
| neutral  | Who is that?  | Wer ist das?    |
| neutral  | I don't know. | Ich weiß nicht. |

The answers *der Mann* and *die Frau* are listed here too — they live in `01-family.md`, where they
double as *husband* and *wife*.

*Wer bist du?* / *Wer sind Sie?* are *sein* in question form. Full conjugation: `04-sein-to-be.md`.
